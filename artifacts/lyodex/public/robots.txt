# Crawling is welcome. The directory, the operator map and the market data exist
# to be found and quoted; a marketplace that blocks the engines people ask for
# recommendations is invisible exactly where it most needs to be seen.
#
# The AI crawlers below are named explicitly rather than left to the wildcard.
# Several of them treat a bare wildcard as covering only their retrieval bot and
# not their training one, or the reverse, so naming each removes the ambiguity.
